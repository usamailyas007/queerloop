# Delete Your QueerLoop+ Account

You can permanently delete your QueerLoop+ account and all associated data directly within the app, in under a minute. No need to contact support.

## How to delete your account

1. Open QueerLoop+ and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap the **Settings** icon (top right).
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**.
4. Review what happens (below), optionally tell us why you're leaving, then tap **Delete my account**.
5. Confirm by tapping **Yes, delete account** in the final warning dialog.

That's it — your deletion request is submitted immediately and you're signed out.

## What happens when you delete your account

- Your profile, posts, reels, photos, videos, captions, comments, and messages are **permanently erased from our active servers within 30 days**.
- Your username is released and can be claimed by another user.
- This action **cannot be undone** and cannot be reversed once confirmed.
- Any safety/abuse reports you previously filed remain in our moderation records to help keep the community safe, but your name and identifying details are stripped from them first.

## Don't have access to the app anymore?

If you can't log in to request deletion yourself (lost access to your email, phone, etc.), email us at **hello@queerloopplus.com** from the address associated with your account (or with whatever proof of ownership you can provide) and we'll process the deletion manually.

## Questions

Contact **hello@queerloopplus.com** for anything not covered here, including questions about what data is collected in the first place — see our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
