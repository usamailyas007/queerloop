# Contact Us

QueerLoop+ does not currently have a dedicated in-app "Contact Us" screen — support and legal inquiries are handled through the contact details embedded in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) and [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) screens.

## Reach Us

**Community Support & Privacy**
📧 hello@queerloopplus.com

Use this email for:

- General support questions and feedback
- Privacy Policy questions or data requests (access, correction, deletion)
- Terms of Service questions
- Reporting violations of our Community Guidelines
- DMCA / copyright infringement claims

## In-App Reporting

For safety and moderation issues, you don't need to email us — use the in-app tools instead, which are reviewed by our moderation team within 24 hours:

- **Report a post or reel:** tap the Safety button on the post/reel
- **Report a comment:** use the Report option on the comment
- **Report a conversation:** use Chat Options → Report Conversation
- **Block a user:** available from their profile or post options menu

## Account & Data Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, go to **Settings → Delete account** in the app. This does not require contacting support — deletion is self-service and data is erased from active servers within 30 days.
